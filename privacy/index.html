Privacy Policy

the analog company · Effective January 1, 2026 · Last updated October 15, 2026

The short version

analog helps independent cafés, bakeries and restaurants remember their regulars. To do that, we hold a small amount of information about the people who visit those venues: how to reach you (a phone number or an Instagram account), a name if you gave one, what you've ordered, and the messages between you and the venue.

We don't sell that information. We don't use it to target ads. A venue only ever sees its own guests, never anyone else's. You can stop messages at any time, and you can ask us to delete everything we hold about you.

The rest of this page is the detail.

1. Who we are and what this covers

The Analog Company ("analog," "we," "us") operates a guest recognition platform used by independent hospitality businesses. This policy covers:

  • Guests: people who tap in at a venue that uses analog, scan a code, text a venue's analog number, or message a venue on Instagram
  • Operators: the venues and their staff who use our app and dashboard
  • Visitors: anyone browsing theanalog.company

When you interact with a venue through analog, that venue is the business you have a relationship with. We handle your information on their behalf and under our own commitments described here. If you want your information removed, you can contact either the venue or us directly.

2. If you're a guest

What we collect

You give us:

  • Your mobile number, if you text a venue
  • Your first name, and last name if you share it
  • Your email address, if you share it
  • Anything you say in a message: a preference, an allergy, a plan to come by, a complaint

If you message a venue on Instagram, we receive from Meta:

  • An identifier for you that is unique to that venue's Instagram account (not your account ID across Instagram)
  • Your username, display name and profile picture
  • The messages, photos, videos, voice notes and other media you send to that venue
  • Story mentions and replies that tag the venue. For a story, we keep a short text description of what it showed, not the image itself.

We receive from the venue:

  • Transaction records from their point of sale: date and time, amount, items ordered, the last four digits of the card used, and a card token used to link a payment back to you
  • Notes or corrections staff make about you
  • Contact details you previously gave the venue directly

We generate:

  • Visit history and timing patterns
  • An approximate distance between your postal code and the venue, if a postal code is available
  • A recognition state (new, returning, regular) derived from the above
  • Records of messages sent, delivered, read and replied to

Where it comes from

You tap a terminal at the counter, scan a code, text a venue's number, message a venue on Instagram, or the venue imports you from their existing contact list.

How we use it

  • To remember you when you visit and reflect that in how the venue talks to you
  • To write and send replies in that venue's voice
  • To offer perks, invitations and other things the venue extends to its regulars
  • To show the venue what's working, in aggregate and per guest
  • To operate, secure, debug and improve the platform

We rely on your consent for messaging. Where GDPR or similar law applies, we also rely on legitimate interests for operating and securing the service.

If you message a venue on Instagram

analog connects to a venue's Instagram professional account through Meta's official messaging tools, with the venue's permission. The venue can disconnect us at any time from its Instagram settings.

  • We only reply to conversations you start. Under Instagram's rules, a venue can reply through analog only within 24 hours of your most recent message to it.
  • Some replies are written automatically in the venue's voice. Venue staff, and in limited cases analog staff, read the conversations. Anything that involves a commitment, such as a complimentary item or a response to a complaint, is held for a person at the venue to approve before it is sent.
  • We use what we receive from Instagram only to run that conversation and the venue's relationship with you. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with any venue other than the one you messaged.
  • To stop, tell the venue in the conversation that you'd like no more messages, or block the account. You can also ask us to delete your data (see section 9).

What we never do

  • We never sell your information. Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to anyone.
  • We never show one venue another venue's guests. If you visit two venues that both use analog, neither one can see the other's relationship with you. This isolation is enforced in our database, not just in the interface.
  • We never use your data to target advertising, ours or anyone else's.
  • We never share your mobile number or your consent to receive messages with third parties for their own marketing purposes. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are excluded from all sharing described in this policy.

Text messages, consent, and stopping them

By tapping in or texting a venue's analog number, you're consenting to receive text messages from that venue at the number you provided. Message frequency varies, typically a few messages a month. Message and data rates may apply.

  • Reply STOP at any time to stop text messages from that venue. We'll confirm once and then stop.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact us at the address below.
  • Opting out of one venue doesn't opt you out of another. Each venue is separate.

3. If you're an operator

We collect your name, work email, phone number, role and venue details; your login credentials via our authentication provider; billing information processed by Stripe (we never see full card numbers); the configuration you give us, such as your voice, your perks and your operating details; and standard usage and device logs from the app and dashboard.

If you connect your venue's Instagram account, we also store an access token for that account, encrypted, along with the account's username. We use it only to receive and send your venue's direct messages. If you disconnect analog from Instagram, we stop using the token and mark the connection inactive.

We use this to run your account, bill you, support you and improve the product.

4. If you're a website visitor

We collect basic analytics: pages viewed, referring source, approximate region, device and browser type. We use these to understand what's working on the site. We don't build advertising profiles from them.

5. Automated replies and AI processing

analog uses large language models to draft replies in each venue's voice. When a reply is drafted, relevant context (the recent conversation, visit history, the venue's own voice samples) is sent to our AI providers for processing.

  • Our AI providers are contractually barred from using this content to train their models.
  • Replies may be reviewed by venue staff or, in limited cases, by analog staff for quality and safety before or after sending.
  • No decision with a legal or similarly significant effect on you is made by an automated system.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and Instagram access tokens are additionally encrypted at the application level. Access is role-scoped and logged. Venue data is isolated at the database level. Payment card numbers never touch our systems: we hold only the last four digits and a token issued by the payment processor.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we'll notify you as required by law.

9. Deleting your data

If you're a guest, email jaipal@theanalog.company with the subject "Delete my data." Tell us the venue you were in touch with and either the phone number you used or your Instagram username. We'll confirm the request comes from you (for Instagram, usually by a message in the same conversation), then delete or permanently de-identify your profile, your messages and any media you sent, and let you know when it's done. We keep only a record that you opted out, so we never message you again.

If you're a venue and you remove analog's access from your Instagram account, Meta notifies us and we disconnect the account straight away. If you or Meta request deletion of data received through that connection, we delete the access token and remove the identifying details of every guest who reached you through Instagram, including names, usernames, profile details and message contents, and give you a confirmation code you can use to check the request's status. You can also email us to request this.

We aim to complete deletion requests within 30 days, and always within the time the law requires.

10. Your rights

Wherever you are, you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what we hold about you
  • Give you a copy of it
  • Correct it
  • Delete it
  • Stop messaging you

If you're in California, the CCPA/CPRA gives you the rights above plus the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and to limit the use of sensitive personal information. We don't sell or share personal information as those terms are defined, and we don't use sensitive personal information for anything beyond providing the service. We won't discriminate against you for exercising any right.

If you're in the EU, UK, or a jurisdiction with comparable law, you also have the right to object to processing, to restrict it, to data portability, to withdraw consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these, email jaipal@theanalog.company. We'll verify your identity, usually by confirming control of the phone number or Instagram account on file, and respond within 45 days.

11. Children

analog is not intended for anyone under 16. We don't knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child's information is in our system, contact us and we'll delete it.

12. Changes

We'll update this policy as the product changes. Material changes will be posted here with a new date, and we'll notify operators directly. Continued use after a change means you accept it.
